— texture and finish

Substantial, creamy

A substantial, creamy oil-in-water emulsion with a more structured texture than the BB cream or toner.

The lipid blend is designed to leave a nourishing, emollient feel after application, making the product particularly appealing to those who prefer richer body care.

— why these ingredients are combined

Humectants attract water, while emollients soften the skin and help reduce moisture loss. Niacinamide, allantoin and the peptide complex add complementary treatment functions. The emulsifying and thickening system gives the formula the body needed to carry a substantial lipid phase while remaining spreadable.
