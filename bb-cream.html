— texture and finish

Light as a veil

The oil-in-water texture is designed to spread easily and distribute colour evenly without the heaviness of a rich cream.

Titanium dioxide and iron oxides provide the tint, helping soften the appearance of uneven skin tone through light coverage. The intended finish is natural and comfortable, allowing the skin’s appearance to remain visible beneath the colour.

— how the formula works

Eight roles,
one fluid

Pigments, emollients, a humectant and botanical extracts, held together by an emulsifying and structuring system.

01

Titanium Dioxide & Iron Oxides

Provide colour and coverage by modifying how light is reflected and scattered at the skin’s surface. Their presence does not establish an SPF or a sunscreen claim.

colour

Ectoin

Selected for its botanical antioxidant profile and its proposed role in supporting skin exposed to environmental stress.

antioxidant

Camellia Japonica Flower Extract

Contributes antioxidant compounds and complements the formula’s skin-conditioning purpose.

conditions

Glycerin

hydrates

Emollient Blend

Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Coco-Caprylate/Caprate and Hydrogenated Polydecene form a complementary emollient blend that improves slip, spreading and softness.

softens

Emulsifying System

Sorbitan Laurate/Polyglyceryl-4 Laurate/Dilauryl Citrate forms the emulsifying system that allows the oil and water phases to coexist within a fluid emulsion.

Xanthan Gum, Cetearyl Alcohol and an acrylic rheology modifier contribute structure and help control flow and pigment suspension.

— why these ingredients are combined

The formula brings together three functions: pigments enhance the appearance of the complexion, emollients support smooth application, and humectant and botanical ingredients add a skincare dimension. The emulsifier and structuring ingredients help distribute these components throughout the product.

— questions

Good
to know

Does it replace sunscreen?

No. Titanium dioxide and iron oxides are in the formula to provide colour and coverage; their presence does not establish an SPF or a sunscreen claim. Use a separate sunscreen when required.

What kind of coverage does it give?

Light coverage that helps soften the appearance of uneven skin tone. The finish is natural and comfortable, and the skin’s appearance remains visible beneath the colour. Build gradually to achieve the desired coverage.

How do I apply it?

Apply a small amount to the face and blend evenly with fingertips, a brush or a sponge, after your skincare routine.

What does it feel like?

A fluid oil-in-water texture that spreads easily and distributes colour evenly, without the heaviness of a rich cream.

Why “anti-pollution”?

Ectoin is an ingredient associated with hydration and protection of cellular structures under environmental stress, and Lespedeza capitata extract was selected for its botanical antioxidant profile and its proposed role in supporting skin exposed to environmental stress. Together with Camellia japonica flower extract they form the skincare rationale behind the anti-pollution concept.
